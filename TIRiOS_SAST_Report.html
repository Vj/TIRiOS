High  TIR-IOS-SAST-015 — Debuggable Entitlement Enabled (get-task-allow = true)
Phase: SAST  |  CVSS v3.1: 7.8  |  MASVS: MASVS-RESILIENCE-2  |  MASTG: MASTG-TEST-0084

Remediation: Sign release builds with an App Store or Enterprise Production Distribution profile where get-task-allow is false.
High  TIR-IOS-SAST-006 — Weak Cryptographic Algorithms or Cipher Modes Detected [ARM64]
Phase: SAST  |  CVSS v3.1: 7.4  |  MASVS: MASVS-CRYPTO-1  |  MASTG: MASTG-TEST-0061
Affected Location: Payload/DVIA-v2.app/DVIA-v2
CC_MD5: MD5 is cryptographically broken (collision attacks) (Use SHA-256 / SHA-3 via CryptoKit); CC_SHA1: SHA-1 is deprecated and vulnerable to collisions (Use SHA-256 / SHA-3 via CryptoKit)
Remediation: Migrate CommonCrypto implementations to Apple CryptoKit using SHA-256/SHA-3 and AES-GCM or ChaCha20-Poly1305.
